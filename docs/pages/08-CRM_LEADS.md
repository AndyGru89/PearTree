# Page 08 — CRM / Leads

**Route:** `/console/project/{id}/leads` and business-level lead views  
**Surface:** Customer/operator Console  
**Priority:** P1

## Goal

Let an operator receive, qualify, assign and progress leads generated by the marketplace.

## Navigation context
Console sidebar includes:
- Dashboard;
- Listings;
- CRM/Leads;
- Messages;
- Calendar later;
- Offers;
- Clients;
- Automations;
- Analytics;
- Settings.

## Lead list
Columns:
- customer/contact;
- requested service;
- location;
- status;
- assigned person;
- source;
- created date;
- last activity.

## Statuses
System baseline:
- New;
- Qualified;
- In progress;
- Won;
- Lost;
- Spam;
- Archived.

Custom pipeline stages may map to stable system states later.

## Filters
- status;
- owner;
- category/service;
- source;
- location;
- date;
- business/location.

## Lead detail drawer/page
- contact;
- request;
- listing/business context;
- consent metadata;
- timeline;
- notes;
- assignment;
- status;
- messages;
- next action.

## Bulk actions
Only for safe operations:
- assign;
- tag;
- archive;
- export where permitted.

No silent bulk destructive action.

## Mobile
P1:
- lead inbox;
- status update;
- call/message;
- notes;
- assignment.

Full analytics/config can remain desktop-first initially.

## Privacy/security
Lead content is personal data.
- tenant scope mandatory;
- RBAC mandatory;
- exports audited;
- no lead body in broad operational logs;
- retention policy applies.

## Analytics
- lead_open;
- lead_status_change;
- lead_assign;
- lead_contact;
- lead_won;
- lead_lost;
- lead_export.

## Acceptance criteria
- cross-tenant lead access denied;
- queue notification failure does not lose lead;
- status change is auditable;
- duplicated incoming event cannot create duplicate lead unintentionally;
- filters remain performant with representative dataset.
