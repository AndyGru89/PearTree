# PearTree.pro — Marketplace Homepage Blueprint

## Goal
Convert visitors into either:
1. successful discovery;
2. account creation;
3. ad/listing publication.

## Page hierarchy

### 1. Header
Desktop:
- logo;
- Browse;
- Categories;
- How it works;
- About;
- optional universal search;
- Sign in;
- Sign up / Post ad CTA.

Mobile:
- logo;
- search;
- menu;
- account/profile.

## 2. Hero
### Left
Headline:
**Buy. Sell. Find Your Next Story.**

Supporting copy:
Marketplace for local people, useful things and trusted services.

Primary interaction:
- search query;
- category;
- location;
- Search.

Popular quick links below search.

### Right
PearTree hero illustration:
- pear-tree symbol;
- marketplace/browser visual;
- clipboard/listing;
- magnifier;
- subtle local landscape.

## 3. Trust strip
Three/four proof pillars:
- Safe & Trusted
- Local Community
- Sustainable Choices
- Easy to Use

## 4. Featured listings
Horizontal/desktop grid.

Each card:
- image;
- premium/featured badge;
- favorite;
- title;
- location;
- price/contact;
- details CTA.

## 5. Browse by category
Icon grid:
- Vehicles
- Real Estate
- Electronics
- Home & Garden
- Fashion
- Sports & Hobbies
- Pets
- Jobs
- Services
- Baby & Kids
- Business
- Other

## 6. Why PearTree
Explain:
- value;
- sustainability;
- locality;
- safety.

## 7. Seller CTA
Large green panel:
**Post an Ad in Minutes**

Include:
- short benefit;
- visual asset;
- primary CTA.

## 8. Latest listings
SEO-friendly listing feed with crawlable detail links.

## 9. Content/SEO layer
Below listings:
- guides;
- popular categories;
- popular cities;
- marketplace safety;
- selling tips.

Do not bury important commercial SEO pages in footer only.

## 10. Footer
- categories;
- help;
- safety;
- blog;
- company;
- legal;
- social;
- language/region.

## UX constraints
- search must remain the dominant action;
- hero illustration cannot obscure form;
- cards must use real content hierarchy;
- page must remain useful without animation;
- above-the-fold CLS target should be minimal.
