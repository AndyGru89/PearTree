# PearTree.pro — SEO Measurement

## Principle
SEO is an acquisition channel, not a traffic contest.

## PearTree.pro funnel metrics
Track by landing page/query cluster:
- impressions;
- clicks;
- CTR;
- qualified sessions;
- signup/demo conversion;
- activation;
- first project created;
- first project published;
- paid conversion;
- assisted pipeline/revenue.

## Tenant SEO metrics
Where product scope permits:
- indexed canonical URLs;
- excluded/noindex URLs;
- sitemap discovered/indexed ratio;
- clicks/impressions;
- listing/category/location performance;
- leads from organic;
- lead conversion;
- pages with zero inventory;
- duplicate/canonical anomalies.

## Technical health
Monitor:
- 404/5xx;
- redirect chains;
- incorrect canonicals;
- accidental noindex;
- orphan pages;
- sitemap invalid URLs;
- duplicate title/H1;
- slow templates;
- uncontrolled parameter crawl.

## Reporting cadence
- technical critical alerts: near-real-time/daily;
- indexation health: weekly;
- content/query performance: monthly;
- revenue/activation impact: monthly/quarterly.

## Decision rule
Do not scale a page template because it gets impressions alone. Scale when:
- indexation is healthy;
- users engage;
- the template produces meaningful business outcomes;
- content quality remains strong as coverage expands.
