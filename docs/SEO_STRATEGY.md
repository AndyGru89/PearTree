# PearTree.pro — SEO Strategy

## Scope

SEO has two separate products:

1. **PearTree.pro acquisition SEO** — acquire operators/agencies/customers for PearTree SaaS.
2. **Tenant SEO Engine** — help customer portals generate technically correct, useful, indexable pages.

These must not be mixed.

## Search principles

Google's current Search Essentials emphasize:
- helpful, reliable, people-first content;
- language users actually search for in prominent page elements;
- crawlable links;
- technically accessible/indexable content.

Google also explicitly treats mass generation of low-value pages as scaled content abuse, regardless of whether the content is generated with AI.

References:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content
