# PearTree.pro — Poland Keyword Research Baseline

**Research date:** 2026-09-25  
**Market:** Poland / Polish language  
**Method:** current Polish SERP and competitor footprint research. Exact monthly volumes are intentionally not invented without a dedicated keyword database. Demand is classified qualitatively from live category depth, competitor inventory and commercial intent.

## Executive conclusion

For the first Polish launch, PearTree should focus acquisition content around four overlapping intents:

1. **oprogramowanie do katalogu firm / portalu branżowego**;
2. **platforma marketplace / marketplace usług**;
3. **generowanie i sprzedaż leadów**;
4. **SEO dla katalogów, marketplace i stron lokalnych**.

The strongest first reference vertical for product validation remains **local/home services lead generation**, because it combines dense local intent, high service values, repeatable category × location architecture and clear provider monetization.

## Evidence from current Polish platforms

### Home/local services
Fixly currently exposes hundreds of thousands of service providers and public category/location structures. Its homepage shows large cumulative inquiry counts for categories such as apartment renovation, appliance repair, emergency plumbing, tile laying and house cleaning.

Observed examples:
- remont mieszkania: over 220k displayed historical inquiries;
- naprawa sprzętu AGD: over 239k;
- pogotowie hydrauliczne: over 176k;
- układanie płytek: over 177k;
- sprzątanie mieszkań i domów: over 174k.

Fixly also states over 1.2m registered customers on its provider acquisition pages.

Implication: this vertical clearly supports category, locality, provider profile, price/guide and lead-capture page types.

Sources:
- https://www.fixly.pl/
- https://fixly.pl/zlecenia/remonty-budynkow
- https://pomoc.fixly.pl/hc/pl/articles/34663610946589-Jak-dzia%C5%82a-Fixly

### Beauty/wellness
Booksy has extremely dense local supply and explicit service × city landing pages.

Observed Warsaw examples:
- Fryzjer Warszawa: 3205 salons/providers shown;
- Salon Kosmetyczny Warszawa: 3736 results shown.

Implication: demand and supply are strong, but competitive intensity and booking-product expectations are also high.

Sources:
- https://booksy.com/pl-pl
- https://booksy.com/pl-pl/s/fryzjer/3_warszawa
- https://booksy.com/pl-pl/s/salon-kosmetyczny/3_warszawa

### Tutoring/education
e-korepetycje shows deep subject × city × exam pages.

Observed Warsaw examples:
- mathematics + matura: around 3466 tutors shown;
- English + matura: over 2200 tutors shown.

Implication: excellent SEO taxonomy and recurring seasonal demand, with lower transactional complexity than accommodation or real estate.

Sources:
- https://www.e-korepetycje.net/mazowieckie/warszawa/matematyka%2Cmatura
- https://www.e-korepetycje.net/mazowieckie/warszawa/jezyk-angielski%2Cmatura

### Accounting / B2B professional services
Oferteo currently shows over 2200 accounting/bookkeeping firms in Warsaw for its accounting category and uses a quote-request lead model.

Implication: high-value lead generation and B2B ARPU potential; local pages plus specialization pages can work well.

Source:
- https://www.oferteo.pl/biuro-rachunkowe/warszawa

### Automotive services
DobryMechanik uses service × city pages with promoted workshops, reviews and booking actions.

Implication: strong local intent and high commercial value; category schema is repeatable across workshop services.

Source:
- https://dobrymechanik.pl/uslugi/mechanika-pojazdowa/warszawa/

### Wedding/event services
Wesele z Klasą exposes supplier profiles, location, price ranges, capabilities and review-driven discovery.

Implication: high order values and strong content surface, but demand is episodic/seasonal and trust/portfolio requirements are high.

Source:
- https://www.weselezklasa.pl/

### Pet services
Polish petsitter platforms expose city/service discovery and direct contact/booking patterns.

Implication: attractive lightweight marketplace use case, but average lead value is lower than renovation, accounting or wedding services.

Sources:
- https://petsitters.pl/
- https://www.petsitter.pl/

### Accommodation
Nocowanie.pl exposes dense destination × accommodation-type pages and direct booking.

Observed Warsaw apartment page: over 1000 objects shown.

Implication: huge SEO surface and monetization, but transaction, availability, pricing and competitive complexity are high.

Source:
- https://www.nocowanie.pl/noclegi/warszawa/apartamenty/

### Real estate
Otodom remains a major marketplace benchmark with deep inventory and market-data content. In 2026 UOKiK publicly opened an explanatory proceeding concerning changes to Otodom terms/pricing, illustrating both the scale and commercial sensitivity of this category.

Implication: valuable but not a good first PearTree reference vertical because of incumbent strength, data/feed complexity and commercial/regulatory sensitivity.

Sources:
- https://media.otodom.pl/
- https://www.uokik.gov.pl/public/zmiany-warunkow-korzystania-z-portalu-otodom-prezes-uokik-mowi-sprawdzam

## Intent model

### High commercial intent
Product/category software terms, comparison/alternative terms, white-label, marketplace software, directory software, lead platform.

### Commercial investigation
How-to monetization, marketplace costs, build-vs-buy, WordPress vs SaaS, marketplace architecture.

### Informational with strong assisted conversion
SEO, taxonomy, lead pricing, portal launch, marketplace metrics and directory monetization.

## Research rule

Before content production, enrich this baseline with a dedicated SEO dataset containing:
- monthly search volume;
- trend/seasonality;
- CPC;
- keyword difficulty/competition proxy;
- SERP features;
- ranking domains.

The live SERP evidence in this file validates market structure, not exact keyword volume.
