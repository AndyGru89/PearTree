# PearTree.pro — SEO Content Plan

## Publishing principle
Build topic authority around launching and operating portals/marketplaces, not generic entrepreneurship content.

## Initial pillar pages

### Build a marketplace
Core guide covering model selection, supply/demand, listings, payments, leads, SEO and launch.

### Build a business directory
Directory-specific model, data acquisition, profiles, categories, locations and monetization.

### Marketplace monetization
Subscriptions, commissions, promoted listings, lead fees and hybrid models.

### Marketplace SEO
Technical architecture, listing pages, category/location pages, internal links, structured data and programmatic SEO.

### Multi-project portal operations
How agencies/operators manage many sites, domains, roles, analytics and shared infrastructure.

## High-intent commercial pages
- marketplace builder;
- directory software;
- local portal software;
- white-label marketplace platform;
- marketplace CRM;
- marketplace SEO platform;
- multi-site portal platform.

## Comparison pages
Create only when PearTree can make a fair factual comparison and keep it current:
- PearTree vs Sharetribe;
- PearTree vs Brilliant Directories;
- PearTree vs Softr;
- PearTree vs Webflow for directories.

Each comparison includes:
- intended user;
- core model;
- key capabilities;
- extensibility;
- pricing source/date;
- situations where the competing product may fit better;
- migration considerations.

## Tool-led content
Potential linkable assets:
- marketplace revenue calculator;
- directory monetization calculator;
- MRR/ARR calculator;
- lead-value calculator;
- portal launch checklist;
- SEO indexability checker;
- listing-page schema tester.

## First 30-content roadmap
Mix:
- 5 pillar/commercial pages;
- 8 use-case/vertical pages;
- 5 comparison pages;
- 6 tactical guides;
- 3 templates/checklists;
- 3 calculators/tools.

Do not publish 30 pages at once simply for volume. Prioritize by customer value, search intent and ability to produce a genuinely useful resource.

## Editorial quality gate
Every article/page must answer:
- What new value does this add?
- What original example/data/tool does PearTree contribute?
- Is search intent clearly satisfied?
- Is it written for a real operator?
- Does it have a logical next action?
- Would we publish it if Google did not exist?
