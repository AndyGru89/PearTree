# PearTree.pro — Poland Vertical Opportunity Scorecard

**Date:** 2026-09-25  
**Purpose:** choose the first reference vertical for PearTree without locking the platform architecture to one industry.

## Scoring model

Each dimension: 1–5.

- **SEO surface** — repeatable useful category/location/provider queries.
- **Lead value** — economic value of a qualified inquiry.
- **Supply fragmentation** — many independent providers who need discovery.
- **Monetization fit** — subscriptions, promoted listings, lead fee or booking.
- **Implementation simplicity** — higher score means easier first implementation.
- **Regulatory simplicity** — higher score means fewer special compliance constraints.
- **Competitive room** — higher score means easier to differentiate from entrenched marketplace products.

Weighted score is directional, not a financial forecast.

## Recommended validation order

### Wave A
**Home/renovation services** and **accounting/B2B services**.

Reason:
- both support lead-first MVP;
- no need for marketplace escrow/availability engine;
- high provider value;
- strong city/category structure;
- easy monetization through subscriptions, promotion and leads.

### Wave B
Wedding/events, automotive, tutoring.

These are excellent second verticals because the same listing + lead + profile + local SEO core can be reused with domain-specific attributes.

### Wave C
Pet services and beauty.

Good marketplace demonstrations, but beauty in particular has a strong incumbent booking ecosystem and therefore requires stronger differentiation.

### Later
Accommodation and full real estate.

Do not use these as the first proof vertical because availability, transactional flows, feeds, inventory freshness and entrenched competitors materially raise implementation risk.

## First reference vertical decision

**Default product-validation vertical: Home & renovation services.**

This is not a claim that it is the largest market. It is the best current architectural validation target because:
- Fixly demonstrates dense provider/customer activity and many high-intent service categories;
- service × city pages map directly to PearTree Listings + Leads + SEO;
- qualified leads have tangible value;
- provider onboarding is straightforward;
- the product can validate promoted listings/subscription/lead monetization without needing marketplace payments.

## Second validation vertical

**Accounting / B2B professional services.**

Why:
- high lead value;
- recurring customer relationships;
- B2B customers can support higher ARPU;
- filters can include business type, accounting form, payroll, tax support and remote/local service;
- strong upsell path to CRM/automation.
